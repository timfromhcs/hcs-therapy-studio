# Code of conduct

## Scope

This is a wellbeing-adjacent product. People arrive with real distress. The
conduct rules therefore cover both the codebase and any support channel.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume competence and good faith; critique the code, not the person.
- Be precise about what is verified and what is a guess.
- Respect that contributors may have lived experience of mental-health
  difficulty. Do not ask for details, do not use it as an argument, do not
  speculate about it.
- Give and accept technical disagreement without escalation.
- Keep discussions free of clinical advice. Nobody here is speaking as a
  clinician, and nobody here may give advice about another person's treatment.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or sustained disruption.
- Using the project to present clinical advice as if it were engineering.
- Pressuring anyone to disclose personal mental-health information.
- Shipping claims that the evidence does not support — including "safe",
  "clinically validated", "diagnostic", or "cures".
- Sexualised attention, slurs, or personal attacks of any kind.

## Reporting

Report privately to the repository owner (`hcsmedia`). Reports are read by the
maintainer; the reporter's identity is not shared. A response is expected within
seven days.

## Consequences

Warnings, then removal of access, proportionate to the behaviour. Deliberate
fabrication of capability or safety results is treated as an immediate removal:
misleading users about a wellbeing tool's guarantees is a safety issue, not a
style issue.
